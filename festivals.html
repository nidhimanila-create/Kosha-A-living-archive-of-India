FESTIVAL OF LIGHTS

Diwali

Diwali, often called the Festival of Lights, is celebrated across India and by Indian communities around the world. Homes and public spaces are decorated with lamps and lights, families gather together, and prayers, sweets and festive meals are an important part of many celebrations.

Why it matters: Diwali represents light, hope, renewal and the coming together of families and communities.

FESTIVAL OF COLOURS

Holi

Holi is a colourful festival associated with spring. People gather to play with colours, share food and sweets, meet friends and relatives and celebrate together.

Why it matters: Holi encourages togetherness, joy, forgiveness and the celebration of new beginnings.

DEVOTION & ART

Durga Puja

Durga Puja is a major celebration dedicated to Goddess Durga. Elaborately decorated spaces, artistic idols, music, rituals and community gatherings are important parts of the festival.

Why it matters: It brings together devotion, craftsmanship, performing arts and community participation.

HARVEST FESTIVAL

Pongal

Pongal is a harvest festival celebrated especially in Tamil Nadu. Families prepare traditional dishes, decorate homes and express gratitude for the harvest, nature and agricultural work.

Why it matters: The festival highlights gratitude towards nature, farming communities and the abundance of the harvest.

DEVOTION & COMMUNITY

Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi celebrates Lord Ganesha through prayers, artistic idols, music and community gatherings. Celebrations can include processions and cultural performances.

Why it matters: It connects religious devotion with art, public celebration and community participation.
