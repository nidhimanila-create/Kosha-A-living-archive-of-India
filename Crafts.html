BIHAR

Madhubani Painting

Madhubani painting, also known as Mithila painting, is associated with the Mithila region. Traditional compositions often feature nature, people, animals and cultural or religious themes.

Why it matters: It preserves visual storytelling and artistic knowledge passed between generations.

MAHARASHTRA

Warli Art

Warli art is associated with the Warli communities of Maharashtra. It is recognised for its use of simple geometric forms to represent people, animals, nature and community life.

Why it matters: It demonstrates how everyday life and cultural knowledge can be preserved through visual art.

ODISHA

Pattachitra

Pattachitra is a traditional painting style associated with Odisha. The art is known for detailed compositions, decorative borders and themes drawn from cultural and religious traditions.

Why it matters: It combines storytelling, craftsmanship and visual traditions into a distinctive art form.

PUNJAB

Phulkari

Phulkari is a traditional embroidery practice associated with Punjab. Floral and geometric patterns are created through detailed needlework.

Why it matters: It reflects textile craftsmanship and the role of embroidery in expressing creativity and cultural identity.

TEXTILE ART

Kalamkari

Kalamkari is a traditional textile art involving hand-drawn or hand-blocked designs and the use of dyes. The craft has developed in different forms in southern India.

Why it matters: It combines drawing, textile making and natural dyeing techniques to create detailed decorative fabrics.
