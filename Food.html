SOUTH INDIA

Masala Dosa

Masala dosa is a popular South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It is commonly prepared as a thin, crisp crepe with a spiced potato filling.

Why it matters: It reflects the importance of fermentation, local ingredients and shared food traditions.

RICE DISH

Biryani

Biryani is a fragrant rice dish prepared in many regional styles across India. Different versions use different combinations of spices, herbs and ingredients.

Why it matters: Biryani shows how food traditions can develop differently across regions while sharing common culinary influences.

GUJARAT

Dhokla

Dhokla is a soft steamed savoury food commonly associated with Gujarati cuisine. Fermentation contributes to its light texture.

Why it matters: It represents the variety of vegetarian foods and fermentation techniques found in Indian cuisine.

BIHAR

Litti Chokha

Litti Chokha is a traditional dish associated with Bihar and neighbouring regions. Litti is a roasted wheat preparation often filled with sattu and served with chokha.

Why it matters: It reflects the connection between local ingredients, everyday cooking and regional food traditions.

NORTH INDIA

Butter Chicken

Butter chicken is a well-known North Indian dish featuring chicken cooked in a rich tomato-based sauce with spices and dairy.

Why it matters: It is an example of how regional dishes can travel beyond their original communities and become widely recognised.
